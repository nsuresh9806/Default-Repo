Independent Researcher | AI/ML & AI for Software Engineering

Shanthi Karpurapu is an independent researcher in Generative AI and software engineering, with more than a decade of experience leading and developing test automation for enterprise systems in healthcare, banking, and manufacturing. Her research explores how Large Language Models can automate acceptance testing, support developer productivity, and improve software quality assurance. She also studies efficient deep learning for medical applications and computational methods for spectroscopic analysis.
